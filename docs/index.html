FAQ

Common questions

Can the platform run with no internet connection?

Yes. Every component, including AI models, runs fully offline. Updates are delivered as signed, verifiable offline bundles.

Which file formats are supported?

All major video, image, audio and document formats, including broadcast codecs (ProRes, XDCAM, MXF), MPEG-TS with KLV, GeoTIFF, NITF, RAW camera formats and PDF/Office.

Can we use our own AI models?

Yes. Bring your own models through our model registry and they plug into the same pipelines, search and review workflows.

How does it integrate with existing systems?

REST & GraphQL APIs, webhooks, and connectors for common MAM, NLE, GIS, SIEM and identity systems.

How is pricing structured?

Licensing is based on deployment model, storage and processing capacity. Contact us for a tailored proposal.
